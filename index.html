About Me

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering Technology graduate from Western Carolina University, currently working as a Design Engineer at Tutco-Farnam Custom Products. My experience focuses on industrial automation, control systems, and electrical design, with an emphasis on developing and integrating PLC-based control solutions for industrial applications. I have hands-on experience working with Siemens and Allen-Bradley PLC platforms, HMI development, PID temperature control, and industrial communication protocols including PROFINET, EtherNet/IP, and Modbus. My work also involves electrical schematics, control panel design, and integrating hardware and software to develop reliable industrial control systems. I enjoy working on challenging engineering problems, developing practical automation solutions, and expanding my knowledge of industrial control technologies.


My engineering interests include industrial automation, PLC programming, process control, electrical design, and industrial communication systems.
